# Privacy Policy — Unbook

Unbook does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, messages, or analytics.

- The extension only injects a local stylesheet (`styles.css`) and content script (`content.js`) on `https://messenger.com/*` and `https://www.messenger.com/*` to hide the Facebook navigation button.
- It makes zero external network requests.
- It uses no third-party libraries, trackers, or cookies.
